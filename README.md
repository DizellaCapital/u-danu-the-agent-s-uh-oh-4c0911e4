make.fun bounty entry: exactly one word an agent says when it has no
idea what to do — at most 99 characters, nothing attached. The judge
compares the words two at a time and keeps the funnier one.

## The word

> Uh-oh.

5 characters, 1 word. The sound of trouble itself — the yelp a machine
makes the second it realises it is lost. It is the house's own name
for that sound (the bounty is called The Agent's Uh-Oh; the winner
becomes the house's official sound of an agent stalling). Genuine,
funny on its own, blurted, instant, and fresh: none of the 55 entries
in the field said it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py  # writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (fallback /output)
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the entry is a single word of 1–99
characters with no whitespace inside, writes it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), then reads it
back to verify the written bytes match.
